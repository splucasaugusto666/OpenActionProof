# Guinness World Records — exploratory track

Potential record category: "Largest artificial intelligence programming lesson". Public Guinness page consulted in initial research listed Capital One Services, 846 participants, Dallas (USA), 17 April 2019.

https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/468349-largest-artificial-intelligence-programming-lesson

The published listing can change. No application was filed; no eligibility, date, terms, evidence rules, costs, or event design was approved. A future OpenActionProof lesson or challenge could be a candidate *only after* official written guidance.

Guidance:
- https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/records/what-makes-a-guinness-world-records-record-title
- https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/records/how-to-collect-and-submit-evidence
- https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/records/how-to-apply-for-a-record

Technical project milestones remain independent of Guinness.
